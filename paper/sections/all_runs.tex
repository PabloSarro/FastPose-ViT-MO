\subsection{Why the Non-Adopted Approaches Are Worse}
\label{sec:catalogue:discussion}
\todo{Write once the best configuration is final (decision gate 2026-12-04). Candidate explanations
below are \emph{hypotheses}, not measured; each should be confirmed by a figure or a number before
it is stated in the report.}
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{1pt}
  \item \textbf{Larger input (512 / 640 px) at 2$\times$.} \todo{Compare with the 4$\times$ runs.}
        Hypothesis: LM-O crops are mostly smaller than 384 px natively, so extra pixels add no
        detail, and the larger token grid needs a longer schedule.
  \item \textbf{One network per object.} \todo{} Hypothesis: with the same samples per object each
        per-object network gets $8\times$ to $30\times$ fewer optimiser steps than the shared one
        and cannot share features across similar objects (T-LESS).
  \item \textbf{Context crops, box jitter, augmentation variants.} \todo{} Hypothesis: they move
        the training crops away from the distribution of detector boxes.
  \item \textbf{Absolute rotation, no pretraining, legacy depth.} \todo{} Hypothesis: they break the
        crop-relative parametrisation or throw away the pretrained features.
  \item \textbf{Direct regression versus PnP.} \todo{} Hypothesis: dense XYZ supervision is a richer
        training signal than six pose numbers, and PnP is geometrically exact for the visible
        surface; the dense heads help even direct regression (+0.02 test).
  \item \textbf{\texttt{pnp\_init} and \texttt{dr\_rot\_pnp\_t}.} \todo{} Hypothesis: for symmetric
        objects the regressed pose and the XYZ target disagree unless the target follows the
        symmetry the rotation loss picks; the symmetric XYZ targets remove the disagreement.
  \item \textbf{CosyPose refiner and our refiner on PnP poses.} \todo{} Hypothesis: the public
        refiner was trained with a different renderer and on larger errors; PnP poses leave
        little for a refiner to correct.
  \item \textbf{Ensembling, own detector, RGB-D.} \todo{} Hypothesis: ensembling two seeds averages
        mostly the same errors; the own detector has lower mAP than the default one.
\end{itemize}
