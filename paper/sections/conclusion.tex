\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \ours{}, a multi-object extension of FastPose-ViT that regresses the 6D pose of every
object of a BOP dataset directly from its image crop, with one Vision Transformer shared by all
objects and conditioned on the object identity by a per-object class token. A translation target
normalized by the focal length and the object diameter makes the formulation independent of the
camera and of the object size, and a symmetry-aware rotation loss with translation offsets handles
symmetric objects; the apparent rotation of the original method remains the most important
ingredient (+0.11 AR). Trained on PBR images only, \ours{} is the best direct-regression method
on LM-O (0.668 AR) and YCB-V (0.702 AR), within 0.03--0.06 AR of the best PBR-only RGB methods,
and it loses no accuracy in TensorRT FP16. With our own multi-class detector, the full pipeline
runs at 57 FPS for 8 objects on an A100.

Our main limitations are the depth accuracy of the directly regressed translation, which explains
most of the gap in the 3D metrics, heavily occluded objects, and a depth bias on real YCB-V
images that synthetic training data alone does not remove. Training with the real images of
YCB-V, measuring the latency on embedded hardware, and \pending{T-LESS} are the next steps.
